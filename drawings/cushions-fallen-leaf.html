Pl. 06

Cushions with a fallen leaf

Rounded cushions of moss crowd the sheet, each built from short, spiky leaves. A pale fallen leaf, folded along its midrib, lies across them, drawn in only a few long lines. Lower down, one cushion is made entirely of dots.
