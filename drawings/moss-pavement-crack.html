Pl. 04

From a pavement crack

A low creeping plant drawn from above. Its thin stems spread out from one dense knot, each tiny leaf outlined in pen. At the bottom the stems gather into a narrow bundle where the plant comes up out of the paving, and at the top left one long runner wanders off on its own.
