Commissions and enquiries

Is there a small place you keep going back to?

A wall you pass every morning. The step outside a house you grew up in. A corner of a garden that someone you love once tended.

Shubhangi takes on a few commissions each year. Each one begins with looking, in person where she can and through your photographs where she cannot. Curators, galleries and collaborators are just as welcome to write.

  1. 01Tell her about the place, in as many or as few words as you like.
  2. 02She writes back with questions, a size, and an honest sense of how long it will take.
  3. 03Then she draws, slowly. You will hear from her when it is ready to travel.
